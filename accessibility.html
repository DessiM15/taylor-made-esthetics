Accessibility Statement

We want every client to be able to learn about our services and book an appointment, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Our commitment

Taylor Made Esthetics aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on this website. These guidelines explain how to make web content more usable for people with disabilities, and more pleasant for everyone.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a skip link to the main content.
  • Text and background colors meet the recommended contrast ratios.
  • Images carry text descriptions, and videos have descriptions and a pause control. Background videos do not play if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Headings, landmarks and link text are structured so screen readers can move through a page quickly.
  • The booking window and offer window are announced as dialogs, keep keyboard focus inside while open, and close with the Escape key.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and at 400% zoom without sideways scrolling.

Online booking

Appointments are booked through Boulevard, a third-party booking system that opens inside this site. If any part of the booking process is hard to use, call or text 346-219-3881 and we will book it for you.

Tell us if something is in your way

If you find a part of this site that does not work for you, let us know and we will fix it or offer the information another way.
