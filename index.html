Your skin's new best friend.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where is Taylor Made Esthetics located?

Inside Pearland Town Center at 11200 Broadway St, Suite #28, Pearland, TX 77584. The suite is private, with free parking at the center.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any Book button on this site. Each service opens directly in our online booking page, where you choose a time and confirm. You can also call or text 346-219-3881.

Do you offer services for men?

Yes. Men's facials, grooming, waxing and signature packages are listed under For Him, and every service is performed in a private room.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours notice to cancel or move an appointment so the time can go to another client. Details are shown when you book.

Is there a membership?

The VIP Membership gives you 10% off every service, priority booking and member-only offers. You can cancel or pause anytime.
