About the Sugar Brazilian

The Sugar Brazilian removes all hair front to back using a warm paste of sugar, lemon and water. Because the paste grips hair rather than skin and is removed in the direction of growth, there is less pulling, fewer broken hairs and fewer ingrowns. It is the most comfortable Brazilian for reactive skin.

What to expect

  • A cleanse and light powder, then a warm sugar paste applied against the direction of growth and flicked off with the growth.
  • The paste only sticks to hair and dead skin, so there is less pulling on live skin and less redness.
  • Any leftover paste rinses away with water. No sticky residue.

Before your appointment

  • Hair can be a little shorter than for waxing, about an eighth of an inch, but a quarter inch works best.
  • Arrive with clean skin and no lotions or oils on the area.
  • Tell Taylor about any skin conditions, sunburn or recent peels on the area.

Aftercare

  • Avoid heat, sweating and sun on the area for 24 hours.
  • Exfoliate gently from day 3 onward and moisturize daily to keep hair from trapping under the skin.
  • Rebook every 4 to 5 weeks for the smoothest results.

Is sugaring better than waxing?

It is gentler on sensitive skin, uses only natural ingredients and can remove shorter hair. Waxing is faster on large areas. Taylor offers both so you can choose what suits you.

Is sugaring good for sensitive skin?

Yes. Because the paste bonds to hair rather than skin and is applied at body temperature, it is usually the most comfortable option for reactive skin.
