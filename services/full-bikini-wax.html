About the Full Bikini Wax

The Full Bikini removes hair from the sides and the top of the bikini area and shapes what remains, without going all the way like a Brazilian. It is the most popular choice for swimsuit season and a comfortable first step if a Brazilian feels like too much.

What to expect

  • A quick check of the area and your skin history, then a cleanse and light powder so the wax grips hair, not skin.
  • Hard wax on sensitive areas and soft wax on larger areas, removed in quick, confident pulls.
  • Tweezing of any stragglers and a soothing finish to calm the skin.

Before your appointment

  • Hair should be about a quarter inch long (around 2 weeks of growth). Do not shave in between visits.
  • Exfoliate gently the day before and arrive with clean, product-free skin.
  • Skip tanning, retinol and strong acids on the area for a few days before. Taking an over-the-counter pain reliever 30 minutes before can help with sensitivity.

Aftercare

  • Avoid heat, friction and sweating for 24 hours: no hot showers, gym, pools or tight clothing on the area.
  • Keep the area clean, then start gentle exfoliation after 48 hours to prevent ingrown hairs.
  • Rebook every 4 to 6 weeks. Hair grows back finer and sparser with consistent waxing.

Does waxing hurt?

There is a quick sting on each pull that fades within seconds. Hard wax, a fast technique and a calm room make a bigger difference than most people expect. It gets easier with every visit.

Can I wax if I am on my period?

Yes. Skin can be a bit more sensitive that week, so book another time if you can, but it is safe to proceed.
